Tab & Tally › Whip-round

Collect for a gift, card or leaving do

Set up a whip-round with a target and a closing date. People add their name and amount, and you tick off each contribution once it arrives. A progress bar shows how much is pledged and received. Money never passes through Tab & Tally.

How it works

  1. Create the pot with a target and a closing date.
  2. Share the link. People say how much they are giving.
  3. Tick each one off when you have received the money.

Questions

Does Tab & Tally collect the money?

No. People pay you directly. The tool only keeps track of who has paid.

Can contributors see what others gave?

Not by default. The organiser can choose to show who gave what.
