Tab & Tally › Quiz night

Score the quiz, live

Enter each team's points after every round and share a live leaderboard. There is also a round-by-round grid. Half points, extra rounds and late teams are all supported.

How it works

  1. Add the teams and the number of rounds.
  2. Enter scores after each round.
  3. Share the code so teams can watch the leaderboard.

Questions

Can I use half points?

Yes, scores can be whole or half points.

Can I add a round or team later?

Yes. You can add, rename or remove rounds and teams at any time.
