Tab & Tally

About Tab & Tally

Tab & Tally is a set of small, free tools for groups of friends, families, clubs and teams. Each one works the same way: someone starts a group, shares one code, and everyone is in. No accounts and no sign-up.

The tools

  • QuidsIn splits bills and shows who owes who, using the fewest payments to settle up.
  • Secret Santa draws names so each person sees only who they're buying for.
  • Whip-round collects for a gift and shows who has chipped in. It never holds the money.
  • Tally keeps score for game nights and leagues, and everyone watches the leaderboard live.
  • Killer Pool tracks pub killer (up to 20 players, taking turns) or own-ball killer, with your own lives and house rules.
  • Sweepstake deals teams, horses or numbers to people at random and shows everyone the result.
  • Quiz night adds up each team's score round by round on a live leaderboard.
  • League works out a league table from the results you enter, and can make the fixtures.
  • Pickers picks a name, makes fair teams, shuffles an order or flips a coin. Nothing is saved on our servers.

Free, paid for by adverts

Everything is free to use. Adverts keep it that way. We keep them away from the screens where you enter money.

Good to know

  • We only track who owes what. We never hold or move your money.
  • Figures are only as accurate as what people enter. Check the totals before you pay anyone.
  • Groups are deleted automatically after 30 days, or 3 months if you choose. Read the privacy policy for details.
