How the draw is dealt

Both lists are shuffled, then items are dealt out in turn. If there are more items than people, a few people simply get one extra, and nobody gets two extra.

The draw happens once and can't be redone, so check the names before you press the button.

Ideas for what to draw

  • Tournament teams, such as the World Cup, Euros or Six Nations. Add the teams one per line.
  • Horses for the Grand National or any race meeting.
  • Numbered squares for a Super Bowl or darts night.
  • Drivers, golfers, contestants on a TV show, or anything with a winner.

Getting the numbers to work

If you can, match the number of people to the number of items. With 32 teams, 8 people get 4 each. With 48 teams, 12 people get 4 each. If it doesn't divide evenly, the page tells you some people will get one more, so you can decide whether that's fair for your group.

Running it as it goes

Share the code so everyone can see who has what. The organiser taps a team once to mark it out and again to mark it as the winner. People can check the page to see who is still in.

Money and prizes

Tab & Tally tracks the draw only. You collect the stakes and pay the prize however you like. If you are collecting for a prize pot, a whip-round can track who has paid.

Questions

Is the sweepstake draw fair?

Yes. Both the items and the people are shuffled and the items dealt evenly.

How many teams or people can I add?

Up to 64 of each.

Can I use it for the Grand National?

Yes. Add the horses one per line and the people who are playing.

Can people see the draw?

Yes, everyone with the code can see who has which item. Only the organiser can mark items out or as winners.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up.
