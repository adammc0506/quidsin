Secret Santa name draw, with no sign-up

Run a Secret Santa online in a minute. The organiser adds the names and the draw is made once on the server. Everyone opens the shared link, taps their name and sees only who they are buying for. Nobody, including the organiser, can see anyone else's match.

How it works

  1. Add the names and choose whether people can add wishlists.
  2. Share the link or QR code with the group.
  3. Everyone taps their own name to see who they are buying for.

Questions

Is this Secret Santa generator free?

Yes, free with no sign-up and no email addresses needed.

Can the organiser see who got who?

No. The organiser can free up a mistaken claim but never sees the matches.

Can someone draw themselves?

No. Nobody gets themselves, and no two people simply swap.

Can couples be kept apart?

Yes. Add pairs to keep apart when you create the draw. Neither person will draw the other.

Can people add wishlists?

Yes, if the organiser allows it. A wishlist is visible only to the person buying for you.
