A good Secret Santa needs three things: a fair draw, matches that stay secret, and a budget everyone agrees on. This free generator does the first two for you, and the tips below help with the third.

How the Tab & Tally draw works

  1. The organiser adds everyone's name and chooses whether people can add a wishlist.
  2. The draw is made once, on the server, as a single loop. Nobody can draw themselves, and nobody gets a mini-loop with one other person.
  3. The organiser shares one link or QR code. Each person taps their own name once and gets a private key.
  4. Only that key shows who you are buying for. The organiser never sees the matches, so they can play too.

Setting the budget and the date

Agree the amount before the draw, and put it in the group chat with the link. £10 to £20 is common for office and friend groups, and £5 to £10 works well for large groups or families.

Pick a date for the gift swap and a deadline to open the link. Tell people the draw is one-off, so they should check the names first.

Wishlists

If you switch wishlists on, each person can add a few ideas. Only the person buying for you can see them, so you still get a surprise but nobody has to guess sizes or tastes.

Couples and families

There is no exclusion list yet. If two people should not draw each other, a simple fix is to run the draw with one name for the couple, or to agree beforehand that anyone who draws their partner swaps quietly with someone else.

If someone loses their key

The organiser can free up a mistaken claim so the person can tap their name again. This releases the name only; the organiser still doesn't see the match.

Questions

Is the Secret Santa generator really free?

Yes. It is free, paid for by adverts, with no sign-up and no email addresses needed.

Can the organiser see who got who?

No. Each person gets their own private key. The organiser can free up a mistaken claim but never sees any match.

Can I redo the draw?

The draw is made once when you create it. If you need a different draw, create a new Secret Santa.

How long is my Secret Santa kept?

It is deleted automatically after 30 days, or after 3 months if you tick Keep for 3 months.

Does it work for remote teams?

Yes. Everyone just needs the link, so it suits teams who are spread across different places.
