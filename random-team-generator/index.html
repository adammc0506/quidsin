Tab & Tally

Random team generator and name picker

Picking teams by hand takes time and causes arguments. Type in the names, choose how many teams you want, and Pickers splits everyone up at random in one tap.

Open Pickers

What you can do

  • Split a list of names into teams of a size you choose.
  • Pick one name at random, like a name from a hat.
  • Shuffle a list to decide the order, such as who goes first.
  • Flip a coin or roll dice to settle a decision.

Why people trust it

Results come from your browser's secure random number generator. Nothing is sent to our servers, so there's nothing to tamper with, and nothing is stored.

Good for

Five-a-side, school and club sessions, pub games, office activities, and anyone who needs a quick, fair decision.

When you need more

For a full competition, make a league table. For a game night, use Tally to keep score.

Questions

Is it really random?

Yes. It uses your browser's secure random number generator.

Is anything saved?

No. Everything stays on your phone.

Can I make uneven teams?

If the number of people doesn't divide evenly, some teams will have one more person than others.

Is it free?
