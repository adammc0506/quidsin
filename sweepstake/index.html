Run a sweepstake without the hat

Add what is being drawn (teams, horses or numbers) and who is playing. The draw is shuffled and dealt fairly in one go, and everyone can see who got what. The organiser marks items out or as winners as the event goes on.

How it works

  1. Add the teams, horses or numbers, one per line.
  2. Add the people. Everyone gets a fair share.
  3. Share the code. The organiser marks who is out or has won.

Questions

Is the sweepstake draw fair?

Yes. Both lists are shuffled and the items dealt out evenly, so nobody gets more than one extra.

Can I redo the draw?

No. It happens once, so check the names first.

Does it handle the prize money?

No. It only tracks the draw. You sort the stakes and prize yourselves.
