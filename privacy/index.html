Tab & Tally

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Tab & Tally makes small free tools for groups, such as QuidsIn for splitting bills. This page explains what we keep and why, in plain language.

Who we are

Tab & Tally is run by Line Rate IT in the United Kingdom. You can reach us at hello@tabandtally.co.uk.

What we store

There are no accounts. We never ask for your email address, a password or any bank details. When you create a group in QuidsIn we store:

  • the group name and a unique reference code,
  • the names or nicknames you add for each person,
  • the expenses and payments you record, with amounts and dates.

For Secret Santa we store the draw name, the names you add, who each person buys for, optional budget and date, and any wishlist each person writes. Pairs you ask us to keep apart are used when the draw is made and are not stored. Private links are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, and nobody, including the organiser, can see the whole draw in the app.

For Whip-round we store the pot name and details, the how-to-pay note, the names and amounts people add, and whether the organiser has marked each as received. We refuse notes that look like account or card numbers, and we never hold or move money.

For Tally we store the scoreboard name, the player names you add, and each score change. Nothing else.

For Killer Pool we store the game name, the player names you add, their lives (and ball numbers in own-ball games), and each change made during the game. Nothing else.

For Sweepstake we store the name, the things being drawn, the people's names and who each item went to. Nothing else.

For Quiz night we store the quiz name, the team names, the round names and each team's scores. Nothing else.

For League we store the league name, the team names and the results you enter. Nothing else.

We use this only to run the group you created. Please use first names or nicknames and avoid anything you would not want the rest of the group to see.

Who can see a group

Anyone who has the reference code or link can see and change that group. Share it only with the people in it.

How long we keep it

Groups are deleted automatically after 30 days, or after 3 months if the person who created it ticked "Keep for 3 months". A group can be extended once by a month, never beyond 3 months in total. Deletion removes the group and everything in it. Our database provider may keep short-term recovery copies for up to 30 days after deletion.

On your device

The app saves your recent groups and which person you are in your browser's local storage on your own device, so it can remember you next time. We do not use this to track you, and it is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Hosting and logs

The site runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising

Tab & Tally is free and is paid for by advertising. When adverts are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure them. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your choice first, and you can change it later. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage your ad settings at adssettings.google.com. We do not show adverts while you are adding an expense.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. Because there are no accounts, we find your data by its group reference. Email us the reference and we will delete the group. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Tab & Tally is not aimed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.
