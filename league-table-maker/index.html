Tab & Tally

League table maker with fixtures

Spreadsheets work until someone enters a result wrong and the whole table shifts. This league maker works out the table for you from the results you enter, and everyone can follow it on their phone.

Start a league

What it does

  • Works out played, won, drawn, lost, difference and points automatically.
  • Orders the table by points, then difference, then goals or frames scored, then name.
  • Lets you set your own points for a win and a draw.
  • Generates round-robin fixtures, each team playing the others once, or home and away.
  • Lets you add one-off results that aren't in the fixtures.

Good for

Pool and darts leagues in the pub, office table football, five-a-side, board game seasons, e-sports and any league where you need a quick table.

Running a league

Add the teams or players and choose the points. Tap Home and away for a double round-robin, or play each other once. Tap a match to enter its score, and share the code so everyone can see the table.

Longer seasons

Leagues are kept for 30 days, or 3 months if you tick Keep for 3 months, with one Keep another month extension. For anything longer, check your plans against that limit.

Questions

How are tied teams ordered?

By goal or frame difference, then by goals or frames scored, then by name.

Can I change the points for a win?

Yes, set the points for a win and a draw when you create the league.

How many teams can I add?

Up to 20, and up to 500 matches.

Can I fix a wrong score?

Yes. Tap the match and enter the correct result, or clear it.
