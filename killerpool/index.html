Run a game of killer pool

Set up pub killer or own-ball killer, choose the lives and house rules, and track who is still in. Pub killer supports up to 20 players with two lives, a final shot to win, and the player who pots the black covering the next re-rack.

How it works

  1. Pick pub killer or own-ball killer and set the house rules.
  2. Record each shot. Misses cost a life.
  3. Share the code so everyone can follow who is still in.

Questions

How does pub killer work?

Players take turns to pot any ball to stay safe. A miss costs a life. The last player standing must pot a ball to win.

Who pays for the re-rack?

With the option on, whoever pots the black covers the cost of the next re-rack.
