Split bills with friends, flatmates and trips

QuidsIn tracks shared expenses and works out who owes whom, using the fewest payments to settle up. Share one code and everyone sees the same balances. It only records who owes what and never holds or moves money.

How it works

  1. Create a group and add the people.
  2. Add each expense and who it was split between.
  3. Share the code. Everyone sees the balances and the simplest way to settle up.

Questions

Is QuidsIn free?

Yes. It is free, paid for by adverts, with no sign-up and no daily limits.

Does QuidsIn handle payments?

No. It only records who owes what. You pay each other however you like.

How long are groups kept?

Groups are deleted after 30 days, or 3 months if you choose that when you create one.
