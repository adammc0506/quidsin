Tab & Tally › Pickers

Pick a name, split into teams, flip a coin

Pickers is a set of quick decision tools: pick a name from a hat, split people into fair teams, shuffle an order, flip a coin or roll dice. It runs entirely in your browser and saves nothing.

How it works

  1. Type in the names.
  2. Choose pick, teams, shuffle, coin or dice.
  3. Get a fair result in one tap.

Questions

Is the result really random?

Yes. It uses your browser's secure random number generator.

Is anything saved?

No. Everything stays on your phone.
